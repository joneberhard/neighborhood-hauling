# Review-request SMS snippets

Two copy-paste text templates for asking customers to leave a Google review.
Reviews flow into the LeadConnector / HighLevel reputation widget shown on the
home page and on `/reviews`.

## Before you send
- **Swap the merge fields:** `[Name]` and `[LINK]`.
- **[LINK]:** use the one-tap Google review link. Mou's Google Business Profile
  is `https://maps.app.goo.gl/oN3CrLU3eVEZMi6V9` (opens Maps → "Write a review").
  If Mou claims a `g.page/r/<id>/review` short link in his GBP dashboard, use
  that instead — it drops customers straight onto the star form (one tap, not two).
  Run the raw link through a shortener if you want a cleaner text.
- Send from the number customers already recognize (the business line), so it
  doesn't read as spam.
- One ask per customer. If no reply, a single soft nudge a few days later is fine.

---

## 1. Past customers (helped a while back)
Warmer / memory-jogging, since it's been a bit.

> Hey [Name], it's Mou with Neighborhood Hauling — thanks again for letting us
> haul your stuff out a while back! We're a small local crew and Google reviews
> make a big difference for us. If you've got 30 seconds, a quick word would mean
> a lot: [LINK] Either way, thanks for the support. Keepin' the hood clean 🤙

*~300 characters with a short link (≈2 SMS segments).*

---

## 2. Recent customers (helped in the last day or two)
Strike while it's fresh; references the just-completed job.

> Hey [Name], it's Mou with Neighborhood Hauling — great working with you! Hope
> the space is looking good. If we earned it, a quick Google review would mean
> the world to a small local crew like ours: [LINK] Thanks, neighbor! 🤙

*~260 characters with a short link (≈2 SMS segments).*

---

## Notes
- Keep the 🤙 only if Mou's into it — it matches the brand voice but it's optional.
- For bulk sends, HighLevel can fire these as a review-request campaign and
  auto-insert the review link; these templates double as that campaign copy.
